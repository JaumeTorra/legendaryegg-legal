Privacy Policy for LegendaryEgg

Last updated: September 2026

LegendaryEgg provides content management and video scheduling tools for digital creators.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account via TikTok Login Kit, we access your basic profile information (display name, avatar URL, open ID) via the user.info.basic scope to display your connected account within your LegendaryEgg dashboard.

2. Video Upload and Publishing

When you authorize video publishing permissions (video.upload and video.publish), we use these permissions solely to upload and publish videos you select and schedule to your TikTok account. We do not access or store your personal passwords.

3. Data Storage & Security

Access tokens are encrypted at rest. We never sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. You can disconnect your account at any time.

4. Contact Us

For questions or data deletion requests, contact: support@legendaryegg.com
